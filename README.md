### 🌐 About Navis Protocol
Navis is an advanced autonomous quantitative trading platform designed to bridge the gap between high-frequency macroeconomic data pipelines and real-time on-chain execution. By leveraging multi-country inflation tracking, microstructure data parsing, and proprietary AI signal engines, Navis eliminates human emotional bias from crypto trading.

### ⚡ Core Architecture & Features
* **AI-Driven Strategies:** Automated real-time processing of complex market data structures.
* **Microstructure Analytics:** Near zero-latency signal generation engine.
* **Macro Intelligence:** Multi-country economic and inflation tracking metrics.
* **Autonomous Execution:** Seamless and secure automated risk management.
